# Usage Policy

This usage policy applies to the publicly hosted OpenRailwayMap services available on https://openrailwaymap.app/.

The services are hosted on a best-effort basis using limited resources. As such, a fair use policy applies to usages of the services:
- Usage of the OpenRailwayMap browser application on https://openrailwaymap.app/ is allowed free of charge. 
- Map tiles may be used in third party applications, on the condition that the third-party application is available publicly and without registration. Attribution to OpenRailwayMap must be provided in a clear manner.
- Map tiles may not be downloaded using automated processes or in bulk. Instead, set up OpenRailwayMap locally with relevant data using [the setup instructions](SETUP.md). 
- Usage of the API is allowed both through applications and automated processes. Ensure that the amount of requests to the OpenRailwayMap services is limited.
- Usage of the OpenRailwayMap services requires sending a valid `Referer` header (browser applications) or `User-agent` header (automated processes). These headers may not be faked.
- Users of the OpenRailwayMap services that do not follow this policy may be blocked without notice.
